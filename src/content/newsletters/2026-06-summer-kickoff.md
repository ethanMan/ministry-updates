Dear friends and partners,

A short update before things get busy.

## The community center is funded

Back in January we asked you to pray about the roof on the community center. As of last
week, the project is fully funded. We did not expect it to come together this quickly,
and we are still a little stunned by it. Thank you to everyone who gave.

Construction starts the second week of June, which lines up with our first team arriving.

## Three teams this summer

- **Mid-June** — a group of eight coming to work on the roof
- **Mid-July** — a family team running our two-week day camp
- **Early August** — a small group focused on home visits

If you have been on one of these trips before, you know the rhythm. If you have not and
are curious, reach out — we would love to talk about next summer.

## How you can pray

- Safety for the construction crew and the teams
- That the day camp would be a real doorway into relationship with families here
- Endurance for us; three teams back to back is a lot

We will write again in the fall with stories. Thank you for being part of this.

With love,

**The Man Family**
